# 📈 Risk Management
## Identification, Analysis, and Mitigation Framework

---

## 🚩 Executive Summary
This folder contains the core risk assessment assets of this portfolio. It demonstrates the ability to identify technical vulnerabilities and translate them into business risks using a structured **Risk Register** and **Heat Map** methodology.

---

## 🛠️ Frameworks & Standards
The documentation in this section aligns with industry-recognized risk standards:
* **NIST SP 800-30:** Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments.
* **BSP Circular 808:** Guidelines on Information Technology Risk Management (Philippine Banking Standard).
* **ISO 31000:** Risk Management Principles and Guidelines.

---

## 📂 Primary Risk Assets
* **[Risk Register]:** A comprehensive log of identified threats, vulnerabilities, and current controls.
* **[Risk Heat Map]:** A visual representation of Risk Likelihood vs. Impact to prioritize remediation efforts.
* **[Treatment Plan]:** Documentation of risk response strategies (Avoid, Mitigate, Transfer, or Accept).

---

## 🎯 Key Competencies Demonstrated
* **Qualitative Risk Analysis:** Assessing the severity of threats to organizational assets.
* **Control Evaluation:** Determining the effectiveness of existing administrative and technical safeguards.
* **Business Impact Analysis (BIA):** Mapping technical failures to operational downtime in a financial services context.
