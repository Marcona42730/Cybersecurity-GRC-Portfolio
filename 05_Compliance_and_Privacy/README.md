## 🚩 Executive Summary
This folder serves as the centralized repository for all regulatory compliance and data privacy documentation. It bridges the gap between Philippine legal requirements (**Republic Act No. 10173**) and technical enforcement via **Microsoft Purview**.

---

## ⚖️ Regulatory Alignment (RA 10173)
This section focuses on strict adherence to the **Data Privacy Act of 2012**. 

### **Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)**
Documentation within this section includes a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) that audits the lifecycle of **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** within a financial services environment:
* **Collection:** Procedures for lawful and transparent data acquisition.
* **Storage:** Security controls for protecting data at rest.
* **Disposal:** Protocols for secure data destruction to prevent unauthorized recovery.

**Objective:** To ensure organizational alignment with the standards set by the **National Privacy Commission (NPC)** and to mitigate risks associated with data breaches and regulatory non-compliance.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation (SC-900 Unit 4)
Applied configurations within the **Microsoft Compliance Solutions** suite:
* **Information Protection:** Implementation of Sensitivity Labels (e.g., Confidential, PII, Financial) to automate data classification.
* **Data Loss Prevention (DLP):** Policies configured to prevent unauthorized egress of sensitive banking data.
* **Zero Trust Identity:** Integration with **Microsoft Entra ID** for secure, least-privilege access.

---

## 📂 Evidence & Methodology
* **Lab Methodology:** Use of a Microsoft 365 E5 Sandbox to simulate enterprise-grade compliance monitoring.
* **Risk Mapping:** Documentation of how technical "Secure Scores" translate to reduced legal liability for the organization.
