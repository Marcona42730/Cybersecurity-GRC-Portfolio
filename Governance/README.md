# 🛡️ Governance
## Policies, Standards, and Operational Oversight

---

## 🚩 Executive Summary
This section documents the administrative controls and organizational standards that ensure data integrity and system security. It highlights the intersection of **Leadership Experience** and **Technical Policy Enforcement**, specifically focusing on the transition from high-volume operations to structured cybersecurity governance.

---

## 🛠️ Core Governance Domains
*   **Identity & Access Management (IAM):** Documenting protocols for user provisioning, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and the Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP).
*   **Policy Development:** Crafting enforceable **Acceptable Use Policies (AUP)** and Security Awareness guidelines.
*   **Operational Leadership:** Applying governance to large-scale teams (**40+ agents**) to ensure compliance with internal KPIs and security mandates.

---

## 📜 Regulatory & Framework Alignment
*   **RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012):** Ensuring organizational policies meet Philippine legal standards for data protection.
*   **NIST CSF (Govern Function):** Aligning identity standards with the "Protect" and "Govern" functions of the NIST framework.
*   **ISO 27001 (Annex A.5 & A.9):** Mapping policies to international standards for Information Security and Access Control.
